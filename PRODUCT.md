## Platform

web

## Users

People in and around Biella choosing who will tattoo them. They want to see enough real work to trust an artist, find out what he does, where he is and when he's open, and then get in touch.

## Product Purpose

The website of AL3 Tattoo Studio, the studio of the tattoo artist Sgt. Zippo in Biella. It shows his real work and his studio, says where and when to find him, and leads to a conversation on WhatsApp.

Success: a local choosing an artist writes to him on WhatsApp.

## Positioning

His own hand. He draws each design himself, on iPad with Procreate and an Apple Pencil, and tattoos it himself, from the drawing to the healed skin. No other studio can truthfully show his drawings becoming his healed tattoos.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One public page, Italian only (the English act names "The Studio Space" and "The Art Wall" are the owner's).
- Static site with no backend and no forms: contact happens on WhatsApp or Instagram.
- The privacy notice states what the site really does: it contacts only its own host, with no analytics, session replay or browser storage. Adding any of these means updating the notice first.
- Legal details on the page: P.IVA 02665090029; privacy contact al3tattoostudio@gmail.com. The notice has not been reviewed by a lawyer.
- The archive shows at least 150 works (275 today). The hero and The Art Wall show 130 designs: his 21 drawings and 109 redrawn designs.
- AI is used for one thing only: faithful redraws of his existing tattoos, each from its original photo. Zippo has approved them, and the footer discloses them. Photos, archive works and motion stay real or code-drawn, and no design is ever invented. Any other AI media, such as an AI-extended video, needs the owner's approval and its own disclosure.
- Terms: "Il Tatuatiere", "Sulla pelle", "Solo il disegno", "Stili".

## Product Principles

- Real before impressive: everything shown is his, and nothing is invented to fill a gap.
- Show his hand: wherever possible, connect the drawing to the skin.
- Let the work make the case: people decide on what they can see, not on claims.
- One next step: every path ends in a message to Zippo.
- The owner's words stay as written: approved copy is verbatim, and drafts stay marked as drafts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Reduced motion keeps the site calm but alive: scroll-driven acts stay, ambient loops stop.
- Phones are a primary case, for the visitors as much as for the owner's reviews, and smooth scrolling on them is a requirement.
